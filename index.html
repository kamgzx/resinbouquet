How It Works

01

Reserve Your Date

Contact us to check availability and secure your spot with a deposit. Limited weekly slots due to handcrafted nature.

02

Send Your Bouquet

Local drop-off in Surrey or Canada-wide shipping. We provide clear instructions for safe packing.

03

Design Approval

Flowers are carefully dried and a custom design is created and emailed for your approval.

04

Receive Your Keepsake

Your flowers are preserved in resin, cured, polished, and ready to display forever.
